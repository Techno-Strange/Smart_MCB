# ⚡ Smart MCB – AI Powered Electrical Safety & Home Automation System

## 📖 Overview

Smart MCB is an IoT-based intelligent electrical safety system designed to monitor, analyze, and control electrical infrastructure in real time. The system combines ESP32-based hardware, AI-powered decision making, face recognition, voice commands, and environmental monitoring to provide a smarter and safer electrical management solution.

The project enables users to remotely monitor and control electrical systems while automatically responding to hazardous situations such as unauthorized access, fire detection, abnormal temperature conditions, and motion-based events.
